What you can do

Open and export

  • Open PDF… or drop a PDF on the window; Try the sample opens a made-up one.
  • Export PDF writes the edits into the file as real text, checks it again, and offers the download.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes, on any page.
  • Ctrl++ and Ctrl+− zoom, Ctrl+0 is 100%, Ctrl + wheel zooms at the pointer.

Text

  • Click a box to select it; double-click it, or press Enter, to edit its text. Escape stops editing.
  • The text reflows inside its own box. Drag the box or its handles to move and resize it; arrows nudge it by 1 pt (Shift: 10 pt).
  • The Text panel sets the font, size, bold, colour, alignment, leading and spacing, for the box or one paragraph.
  • Split cuts the box at the caret. Shift-click a second box, then Merge joins it into the first.
  • Delete on a selected box (not editing) clears its text.

Pictures and shapes

  • With Select on, click an object to select the topmost one there. Alt-click, or click again, picks the one below (Alt+Shift: above).
  • Tab and Shift+Tab step through the page's objects in paint order. Enter steps into a group, Escape steps out.
  • Drag to move. Square handles resize (Shift keeps the proportions, Alt resizes about the centre); the round handle turns it (Shift: steps of 15°).
  • Arrows nudge by 1 pt (Shift: 10 pt). R turns right, Shift+R left. Delete removes it.
  • Shift-click adds objects to the selection; Group (Ctrl+G) makes them one, Ungroup (Ctrl+Shift+G) splits one. Logos and charts come grouped (dashed outline).
  • Forward (Ctrl+]) and Backward (Ctrl+[) change the paint order by one.
  • When a shared crop cuts the object, the bar offers to move it with or without the crop.
  • The Objects panel lists what is under the pointer; a click there selects it.

Images

  • Add image puts a PNG or JPEG on the page in view; or drop one on a page where it should go.
